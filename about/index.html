About

Our mission

The operating principle behind every piece of content we create is to make the unknown, known. To decipher signal from noise. By doing so, we mutually improve the results of our business, our clients' businesses, and the resulting judgment and predictive power of our clients' constituents.

Our story

Genesis Content was founded in 2014 by Gerald McDonald. What began as a freelance creative service has evolved into a performance content engine for founder-led businesses and e-learning brands.

Over the years, Gerald has worked in media verticals that give Genesis Content a rare mix of creative taste and commercial discipline. The company was built on a simple belief: content should not just look good or sound smart. It should create measurable business outcomes. Today, Genesis Content helps brands turn their ideas, expertise, and founder story into content systems that build trust, generate demand, and grow ARR.
