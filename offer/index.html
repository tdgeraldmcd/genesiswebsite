04 / A FEW FAIR QUESTIONS

Clear terms.
No guesswork.

What counts as a qualified call?

Before we send anything, we agree on the businesses and people you want to speak with: their industry, company size, role, and any other must-haves. A billable call must meet those criteria and actually take place.

What if they don’t show up?

You don’t pay for that call. If it’s rescheduled, it only becomes billable when the qualified prospect attends.

How much does each call cost?

We agree on a price per qualified, attended call before starting. The price depends on your target market and qualification criteria. Book a call so we can understand your offer and give you a clear price.

Do you guarantee closed deals?

No. We book qualified sales conversations; you handle the sales call and close. You pay for qualified calls that happen, whether or not they turn into customers.
