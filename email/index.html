How this is calculated

The funnel: monthly sends = list size × emails per month. Opens = sends × open rate. Clicks = sends × click-through rate. New clients = clicks × click-to-client rate (the share of clickers who eventually book and buy — deliberately small for high-ticket services, where a sale means a booked call and a closed proposal, not a checkout).

The benchmarks: average open rates across industries run roughly 19–21%, with B2B and professional services in the 18–22% band and strong senders (consulting lists on some platforms) reaching 40%+. Average click-through rates sit near 2–2.5%, with anything above 3.5% considered excellent. The Conservative scenario assumes below-average engagement and a 0.4% click-to-client rate; Benchmark uses industry-average rates and 0.8%; Strong assumes an engaged, well-segmented list at 1.5%.

The cost side: running cost is estimated as a platform fee scaled to list size plus a flat content/labor allowance (~$500/mo if outsourced or valued at your time). The industry-average return of $36–$42 per $1 is shown for reference; your computed ratio appears above and is usually more conservative.

These are directional estimates for planning conversations, not forecasts. Sources: Litmus/DMA ROI studies, Mailchimp, MailerLite, Brevo, WebFX, and monday.com 2025–2026 benchmark reports.
